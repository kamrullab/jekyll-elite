Jekyll Elite is a multipurpose theme system. Choose a layout that fits the page, then connect it to posts, projects, documentation, or front-matter data.

## Included content models

- **Posts** power blogs, technical writing, and magazine stories.
- **Projects** power portfolios, showcases, creative grids, and case studies.
- **Docs** power ordered guides with section navigation.
- **Page data** powers landing, product, resume, and profile layouts.

## Included layouts

Use `showcase`, `profile`, `portfolio`, `project`, `docs`, `landing`, `resume`, `magazine`, `creative`, `home`, `archive`, `post`, or `page` in front matter.
